**Based on:** [architecture definition 1.0](../../architecture/architecture-definition.md), [architecture requirements 1.0](../../architecture/architecture-requirements.md), [security assessment 2026-11](../../security/assessment-2026-11.md)

## Changes from (none)
- First grill. All eight roots are answered; three open questions (Q1–Q3) are left for the owner.

## 1. Problem

The family's files are spread across phones and personal Google accounts, and there is no shared place the family controls. Family Drive gives them one private, Drive-like place for those files. It is being built now so the family owns its data cheaply instead of paying for Google One per person.

## 2. Users

| User | Needs to | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| **Family member on a phone** | Upload and browse photos and files, preview them, and undo their own mistakes | **Primary** |
| Family member on a desktop | Organise the shared drive, import from Google Drive, edit text files | Secondary |
| Owner / operator | Invite people, set access levels, deploy, recover files, keep cost down | Secondary |

Access roles (reader, writer, folder owner, trash manager) are defined in [architecture definition §3](../../architecture/architecture-definition.md#actors-and-roles) and the [glossary](../../../GLOSSARY.md#access).

**Usage today:** not recorded yet (Q1).

## 3. Scope

**Next release: a hardening and trust release, with no backup copy** (see §5 and [ADR 0018](../../adr/0018-no-backup-copy-within-a-two-dollar-budget.md)):
- an AWS Budgets alert on the monthly cost (free tier of AWS Budgets);
- the Content Security Policy enforced rather than report-only (NFR-S4);
- recovery of an earlier version of a file from inside the app, so writers don't need the owner and the console (FR-24).

**Non-goals:** a backend or database (ADR 0001), public share links, offline use (ADR 0007), native apps, other families or tenants, an independent backup copy while the budget is ~$2 a month (ADR 0018).

## 4. Success

| ID | Measure | Target | By |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Monthly AWS cost | ≤ **$2**, with a Budgets alert at $2 | 2026-12-31, then every month |
| M2 | File-recovery requests to the owner that a writer could have done in the app | 0 | 2027-03-31 |
| M3 | Family members active in a month | ≥ N (Q2) | 2027-03-31 |

## 5. Constraints

- **Budget: about $2 a month in total.** This is a ceiling, not a preference. Anything that would push the monthly cost past it is out of scope, however important.
- **People:** one operator (the owner), working in spare time.
- **Platforms:** AWS `ap-southeast-1` in one account; Google is the only identity provider; phones (Android Chrome, iPhone Safari) and current desktop browsers.
- **Regulation and privacy:** nothing beyond keeping family data private to the people allowed to see it.
- Architecture constraints C-1 to C-8 in [architecture requirements §3](../../architecture/architecture-requirements.md#3-constraints-and-assumptions) still hold.

## 6. Quality

Within the budget ceiling in §5, in trade-off order:

1. **Data durability:** never lose a file.
2. **Privacy and security.**
3. **Low cost**, below the ceiling.

Usability comes fourth. The budget ceiling outranks all three: that is why there is no backup copy today even though durability comes first ([ADR 0018](../../adr/0018-no-backup-copy-within-a-two-dollar-budget.md)).

## 7. Domain language

The brief uses the terms in [`GLOSSARY.md`](../../../GLOSSARY.md) as they stand: shared drive, personal folder, Deleted folder, allowlist, access level, access flags, capabilities, location policy. One term is added: **backup copy**, distinct from a version and from the Deleted folder. None exists today.

## 8. Risks and bets

| ID | Risk or bet | Mitigation | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Files are lost with no second copy: a bad foundation pipeline run, a compromised account, or a mistake noticed after 30 days | Versioning (30 days), the Deleted folder, `prevent_destroy`, gated pipelines | Accepted within the budget (ADR 0018) |
| R2 | A family member is locked out of Google import because they aren't a Google test user while the consent screen is in Testing (C-2) | Owner adds them; ADR 0008 has the switch-back path | Watch |
| R3 | A Storage Browser upgrade breaks the custom parts | Feature seam (#40), playground re-check after upgrades | Watch |
| B1 | **Untested bet:** family members will move their photos here instead of Google Photos | Measure with M3 | Test first |
